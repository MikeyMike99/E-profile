3. How Your Data is Handled: Zero Database Architecture

We operate under a strict Zero-Trust and data minimization framework. We do not maintain or operate a database.

  • Local Flat-File Storage Only: Rather than using relational SQL engines or third-party cloud database warehouses, subscriber records are maintained exclusively in lightweight local flat-file storage (subscribers.json) on our secure host system.
  • Minimal Data Stored: We store only the email address you provide, cryptographically random preference tokens, chosen cadence/appetite preferences, and timestamps of recent activity.
  • Zero Telemetry & Tracking: We do not employ third-party tracking cookies, advertising pixels, or analytics trackers.
  • Absolute Non-Disclosure: We never sell, rent, monetize, or disclose your email address or subscriber telemetry to third-party data brokers or advertisers.

4. Subscription Activity Requirement & Inactivity Purge Policy

To keep our local flat-file registry clean and ensure that intelligence briefs are delivered only to readers who actively want them:

  • Periodic Site Visit Requirement: Subscribers are required to visit the website from time to time (by clicking story links in the emails, checking the Accessible Audio Suite, or accessing the preference center) to maintain an active subscription.
  • Presumption of Non-Subscription: If a subscriber does not visit the website from the emails they receive over time, our system presumes that they did not intend to subscribe to the service or no longer wish to receive dispatches.
  • Automated Data Purge for Inactivity: Inactive subscriber accounts with no recorded visits are automatically and permanently purged from the local registry, leaving no residual records behind.

5. 1-Click Unsubscribe & Complete Data Purge Guarantee

You retain complete sovereignty over your inbox and data:

  • Accidental Subscription Relief: If you did not subscribe to this newsletter or wish to stop immediately, every dispatch includes an explicit notice and high-contrast button: "If you have not subscribed to this newsletter, please just hit that pretty button below to relieve your inbox."
  • Single-Click Data Purge: Clicking the prominent red button (or submitting an RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe) immediately purges your email address and configurations from the registry.
  • No Lingering Records: Once purged, no background retainers, analytics profiles, or zombie records remain on disk. You will never receive another dispatch unless you explicitly re-enroll.
