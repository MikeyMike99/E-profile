🎯 What is this tab for?

Welcome! This tab is the central technical archive and engineering portfolio for Logan (Michael Robberts). Here you will find full architectural blueprints, forensic devlogs, zero-trust cybersecurity textbooks, and step-by-step implementation guides for all 6 software systems built across the ecosystem.

📋 Documentation Scope & System Maturity Status

To maintain engineering rigor and avoid ambiguity, documentation across this {{ total_docs }} chapter archive is categorized into two distinct operational scopes:

✅ Verified Production Systems (Full Architecture Specs)
  • Siraugga / DevCore Framework: Complete verification spanning 199 5-Pillar Architecture Devlogs, 50-Chapter Zero-Trust Cybersecurity Textbook, and Master Manual. Active production deployment.
  • E-Profile Portfolio Portal: Full forensic engineering devlog, zero-downtime hot-patching runtime, LibCST AST self-mutation, and dynamic multi-tier RBAC access control.
  • EncryptMe Multi-User Vault: Industrial-grade PBKDF2 symmetric cryptography, split-key recovery (IGAR), memory zeroization, and complete architectural/user manuals.
  • JOSHY Audio Suite: Standalone accessible audio suite, accessible_output2 speech drivers, Tolk DLL bridging, and Nuitka binary packaging guides.
⚠️ Incomplete Snapshots & Conceptual Overviews
  • Echoes of the World (Audio Game): Incomplete Architectural Snapshot. Active, evolving 3D spatial audio game codebase. The documentation preserves key foundational patterns (coordinate spaces, C-DLL screen reader bindings, decoupled state machine) from its development history, but does not represent a finished or production-stabilized software release.
  • The Quantitative Era (Trading Bots): High-Level Conceptual Overview Only. Positioned at the conclusion of the narrative journey. It provides a general retrospective on algorithmic execution models, tick engines, and automated telemetry, but does not contain complete or exhaustive technical documentation due to fragmented historical logs.
