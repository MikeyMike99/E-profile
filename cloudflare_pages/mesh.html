Siraugga Chat

Private access to Siraugga. It needs the Cloudflare One app on your device.

1. Open Siraugga

If the app is already installed and switched on, this opens Siraugga.

Open Siraugga

If it does not load, the app is missing or switched off. Do steps 2 and 3.

2. Install the app (first time only)

Download Cloudflare One

Opens the download page for all devices.

3. Sign in (first time only)

  1. Open the Cloudflare One app and choose Login with Cloudflare Zero Trust.
  2. Enter the team name frosty-grass-595c.
  3. Enter your email and the code that is sent to it.
  4. Switch the app on, then go back to step 1.

Only the owner's email is allowed to join.
