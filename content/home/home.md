## Zero-Trust Architecture & Tiered Access Model

This platform is not a static brochure. It is an active demonstration of a custom-engineered, least-privilege web environment. Content is partitioned into two clear operational tiers:

### 1. Public Tier (Open Access)
* **Executive Profile & Mission (`/bio`):** Background, core philosophy, and advocacy proving the technical capabilities of blind engineers in cybersecurity.
* **Architecture Documentation (`/docs`):** Central design specifications, runbooks, and foundational system references.

### 2. Clearance Tier (Invitation & Token Protected)
To protect proprietary intellectual property, ongoing security research, and verifiable credential assets, the following vaults are gated behind cryptographic access tokens and unique employer links:
* **Proprietary Projects (`/projects`):** Production engines including the Echos Audio Workstation (custom blind DAW architecture), surgical filesystem automation tools, and private repository suites.
